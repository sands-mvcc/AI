Types of Business Ownership

BUS-101 – Introduction to Business

Class Time: 4 Hours

🎯Lesson Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Define and explain the concept of business ownership.
  2. Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of each form of business ownership.
  3. Analyze the legal liability, tax implications, and control structures of various ownership types.
  4. Compare and contrast funding and investment opportunities by business type.
  5. Apply knowledge of business ownership to real-world scenarios and case studies.

🧠Key Concepts

  • Importance of business structure selection – Choosing the right structure affects liability, taxes, and growth potential
  • Ownership and legal liability differences – Understanding personal vs. business liability protection
  • Tax treatment across structures – Pass-through vs. corporate taxation models
  • Decision-making authority and control – Who makes decisions and how power is distributed
  • Funding and capital acquisition – How different structures raise money and attract investors
  • Growth and exit strategies – Scalability and options for selling or transferring ownership

💼Business Ownership Types Overview

Sole Proprietorship

One owner, simplest structure

✓ Easy to start

✓ Complete control

✓ Pass-through taxation

✗ Unlimited personal liability

✗ Limited funding options

Partnership

Two or more owners share responsibility

✓ Shared resources

✓ Pass-through taxation

✓ Complementary skills

✗ Unlimited liability (general)

✗ Potential for conflict

Corporation

Separate legal entity from owners

✓ Limited liability

✓ Easy to raise capital

✓ Perpetual existence

✗ Double taxation (C-Corp)

✗ Complex regulations

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

Hybrid structure combining benefits

✓ Limited liability

✓ Flexible taxation

✓ Less formal than corporation

✗ Varies by state

✗ Self-employment tax

Cooperative

Member-owned and democratically controlled

✓ Democratic control

✓ Shared profits

✓ Community focus

✗ Slower decision-making

✗ Limited individual profit

Franchise

Business model using established brand

✓ Established brand

✓ Support & training

✓ Lower risk

✗ High initial costs

✗ Limited flexibility

📝Suggested Assignments and Activities

In-Class Activities

1. Business Ownership Comparison Table

Students fill out a matrix comparing all business types on:

  • Liability
  • Taxation
  • Decision-making
  • Capital
  • Example business

2. Ownership Role-Play Exercise

Groups simulate different ownership types making a business decision (e.g., expanding to a second location)

  • Assign each group a different ownership structure
  • Present the same business scenario to all groups
  • Compare how decision-making differs by structure

3. Kahoot or Quizizz Game

Quick interactive quiz to reinforce key concepts

  • 20-30 questions covering all ownership types
  • Include scenario-based questions
  • Award points for speed and accuracy

4. Real Business Research

Assign each group a real business and have them identify and explain the ownership type

  • Subway – Franchise
  • REI – Cooperative
  • Apple – Corporation
  • Local small businesses – Various structures

📚 Homework Assignments

Written Reflection (1 page):

"Which form of business ownership best suits your future goals and why?"

  • Discuss personal career aspirations
  • Analyze which structure aligns with goals
  • Consider pros and cons for your specific situation
  • Reference concepts learned in class

Research Assignment:

Interview a small business owner and report on why they chose their ownership structure.

  • Find a local business owner willing to be interviewed
  • Prepare 5-10 questions about their business structure
  • Write a 2-3 page report on your findings
  • Include what you learned and how it relates to class material
