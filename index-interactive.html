About This Workshop

Course Overview

Everything you need to bring AI confidently into your classroom.

AI Fundamentals for Educators is an intensive professional development workshop designed for educators at every level who want to confidently integrate artificial intelligence into their classrooms. Hosted by the National Cybersecurity Training and Education Center (NCyTE) as part of the Virtual Faculty Development Weeks (vFDW), this workshop bridges theory and practice — moving from foundational AI and LLM concepts through hands-on creation of AI-powered course materials, assessments, instructional graphics, and classroom tools.

Participants explore leading AI platforms, master prompt engineering techniques, build GitHub-hosted repositories, and develop an AI Acceptable Use Policy tailored to their discipline. No prior AI experience required. Every module produces real deliverables ready to use day one.

What You'll Learn

Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

  1. 1

    Define artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and large language models, and explain their growing role in education and the workforce.

  2. 2

    Evaluate leading AI tools including MS Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Napkin, Mapify, NotebookLM, Gamma, and Goblin Tools for classroom applicability and pedagogical fit.

  3. 3

    Design effective AI prompts and meta-prompts using structured frameworks to generate accurate, discipline-specific educational content and custom AI interfaces.

  4. 4

    Create AI-generated instructional materials tailored to diverse learner needs, including lesson plans, assessments, rubrics, instructional graphics, and multimedia resources.

  5. 5

    Build a GitHub Pages repository hosting AI-powered educational tools, interactive learning activities, and embeddable content accessible via iFrames.

  6. 6

    Develop a classroom AI Acceptable Use Policy grounded in ethical principles, bias awareness, academic integrity standards, and data privacy requirements.
