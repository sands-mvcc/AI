Agentic Browsers

A highly interactive lesson on the future of web navigation.

1. The Paradigm Shift

Traditional browsers display data. Agentic browsers do work.

🖱️

Traditional

User reads content.

User decides where to click.

User fills out forms manually.

🤖

Agentic

AI scans the DOM (code).

AI plans the workflow.

AI executes clicks and typing.
