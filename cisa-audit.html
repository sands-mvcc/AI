    Domain 2 reference

    QA vs. QC vs. QMS
    QC (Quality Control): operational, in-process checks that catch defects before delivery — testing a build, reviewing code. Owned by the delivery team.
    QA (Quality Assurance): process-level assurance that QC and development activities are actually working as intended — an independent check on the process, not the product. Must be organizationally independent of the function it assesses to be credible.
    QMS (Quality Management System): the overarching, documented system of policies, roles, and continuous-improvement mechanisms (e.g., ISO 9001, CMMI) that governs how an organization plans, controls, and improves quality across all of IT — QA and QC are components within it.
    Independence of the QA function
    QA loses credibility when it reports through the same management chain it is assessing — mirrors the auditor-independence principle. A QA lead reporting to the delivery manager whose schedule QA might jeopardize creates a structural conflict of interest, regardless of the individuals' integrity.
    PDCA / continuous improvement
    Plan → Do → Check → Act. A quality program without a real "Act" step (using findings to change the process) is just measurement, not improvement. Watch for organizations that "Check" (dashboards, metrics) but never close the loop.
    Maturity models
    Frameworks (e.g., CMMI-style levels) describing how repeatable, defined, managed, and optimized a process is. Self-reported maturity should be corroborated with evidence — a "Level 4: Quantitatively Managed" claim needs independent verification and a real improvement loop, not just a dashboard.
    Strategic alignment of quality objectives
    Quality KPIs are only meaningful if they trace back to the organization's actual strategic objective. Velocity and defect-count trends measure delivery mechanics; they are not the same as outcome metrics (adoption, customer impact, regulatory compliance) the strategy actually depends on. A metric that looks good but doesn't move the strategic needle is a vanity metric.
