Scenario: The Syllabus Rescue Mission

The Mission: You are designing a prompt to generate a syllabus for a controversial course: "The Ethics of AI Surveillance." A standard prompt produced generic, biased results. Build an Advanced Prompt below to fix it.

1

Assign the Persona

The generic output sounded like a Wikipedia article. We need deep academic expertise.

Option A: Standard Role

"You are a helpful assistant who knows about AI."

Option B: Expert Domain Persona

"Act as a Tenured Professor of Data Privacy Law & Ethics with 20 years of curriculum design experience."

2

Select Reasoning Strategy

The topic is complex. We need to prevent the AI from rushing to a shallow answer.

Option A: Tree of Thoughts (Branching)

"Adopt three perspectives (Lawyer, Sociologist, Technologist). Debate the key topics before deciding the schedule."

Option B: MIT Style (Decomposition)

"Break the problem into sub-tasks: 1. Learning Objectives, 2. Readings, 3. Assignments. Solve sequentially with confidence scores."

3

Add Verification Layer

AI often hallucinates book titles. How do we ensure the reading list is real?

Option A: No Verification

"Ensure the list is accurate." (Trusting the model blindly)

Option B: Chain of Verification

"After drafting, stop. Cross-reference every citation against your internal database. If confidence is < 1.0, remove it."
