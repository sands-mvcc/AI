Horse Breeds & Classifications

The modern horse (Equus ferus caballus) is a masterpiece of selective breeding. From the massive Shire bred for pulling plows to the aerodynamic Thoroughbred bred for speed, every breed follows the rule of "Form to Function."

Student Learning Objectives

  • Classify horses into Light, Draft, and Pony categories.
  • Distinguish between Hotblood, Coldblood, and Warmblood temperaments.
  • Identify 15 common breeds based on conformation and origin.
  • Calculate height correctly using the "Hand" unit.
