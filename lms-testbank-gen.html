Principles in play

This tool follows established assessment-design conventions so your bank is usable from day one.
  • Items align to objectives when supplied — every question carries an objective tag.
  • Reading complexity calibrates to the selected student level automatically.
  • Distractors are written to be plausible — no joke options, no trick wording.
  • Difficulty mixes recall, application, and analysis when "mixed" is chosen.
  • All AI output is labeled draft material — educator review is required before classroom or LMS use.
  • Do not upload copyrighted source material unless you hold rights to use it.
