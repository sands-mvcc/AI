What is Modulus?

Hi Class! 👋 Have you ever noticed that after 12:00 on a clock, we don't say 13:00? We start over at 1:00!

Modulus is just a fancy word for "Remainder Math" or "Clock Math." It answers the question: "Where will the hand land after spinning around?"

The Rule:

A % B = R

  • A is the total number (Total hours passed).
  • B is the clock size (usually 12).
  • R is the Remainder (Where the hand lands).
15 % 12 = 3

"15 divided by 12 is 1, with a remainder of 3"

It's 12:00. Wait 15 hours.
