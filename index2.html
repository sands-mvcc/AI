About This Workshop

Course Overview

Everything you need to bring AI confidently into your classroom.

AI Fundamentals for Educators is an intensive five-day professional development workshop designed for educators at every level who want to confidently integrate artificial intelligence into their classrooms. Hosted by the National Cybersecurity Training and Education Center (NCyTE) as part of the Virtual Faculty Development Weeks (vFDW), this workshop bridges theory and practice — moving from foundational AI concepts to hands-on creation of AI-powered course materials, assessments, and classroom tools.

Participants will explore leading AI platforms, master prompt engineering techniques, and develop an AI Acceptable Use Policy tailored to their own discipline. No prior AI experience is required. Participants leave with real deliverables ready to implement on day one back in the classroom.

What You'll Learn

Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

  1. 1

    Define artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning, and explain their growing role in education and the workforce.

  2. 2

    Evaluate leading AI tools and large language models — including MS Copilot, ChatGPT, and Claude — for classroom applicability and pedagogical fit.

  3. 3

    Design effective AI prompts using the CO-STAR framework to generate accurate, discipline-specific educational content.

  4. 4

    Create AI-generated instructional materials tailored to diverse learner needs, including lesson plans, assessments, study guides, and multimedia resources.

  5. 5

    Integrate AI tools into existing teaching workflows, including personalized feedback, accessibility supports, and interactive course chatbots.

  6. 6

    Develop a classroom AI Acceptable Use Policy grounded in ethical principles, bias awareness, academic integrity standards, and data privacy requirements.
