John Sands

Professor Cybersecurity

John Sands is a prominent Professor of Cybersecurity at Moraine Valley Community College. He serves as the Director and Principal Investigator of the Center for Systems Security and Information Assurance (CSSIA), a National Science Foundation (NSF) Advanced Technological Education (ATE) National Resource Center. He is actively involved in leading national initiatives to improve cybersecurity education and faculty development across the United States.
